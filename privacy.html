Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 25, 2026

Introduction

Finzy ("we", "our", or "the app") is a third-party Jellyfin client that allows you to access and stream content from your Jellyfin Media Server. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Authentication Information

When you sign in to Finzy using your Jellyfin account, we collect and process:

  • Jellyfin authentication tokens
  • Jellyfin account username
  • Jellyfin server connection information

Local Network Information

To connect to your Jellyfin Media Server on your local network, we may access:

  • Local network device discovery information
  • IP addresses of Jellyfin servers on your network

Usage Information

The app stores locally on your device:

  • Your authentication session
  • App preferences and settings
  • Recently accessed content history

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate you with your Jellyfin account
  • Connect to and communicate with your Jellyfin Media Server
  • Display your media library and stream content
  • Maintain your session and app preferences

Data Storage and Security

  • Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using platform-specific secure storage mechanisms
  • App preferences are stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to our servers — all communication is directly between your device and your Jellyfin Media Server

Third-Party Services

Jellyfin

Finzy connects directly to your Jellyfin Media Server. When you use this app:

  • You authenticate directly with your Jellyfin server
  • Your media streaming occurs between your device and your Jellyfin Media Server

Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your information with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Transmit your data to our own servers
  • Track your viewing habits outside of local app functionality

Your data is only shared with:

  • Your Jellyfin server for authentication and media streaming (as required for the app to function)

Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • Sign out at any time to remove your authentication session from the device
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Manage your Jellyfin account directly through your server's dashboard

Children's Privacy

Finzy does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app relies on Jellyfin account authentication, and users must comply with Jellyfin's terms of service and age requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

Data Retention

  • Authentication tokens are retained on your device until you sign out or uninstall the app
  • Local preferences and settings are retained until you uninstall the app
  • No data is retained on our servers as we do not operate any backend services

International Data Transfers

As we do not operate backend servers, there are no international data transfers from our side. Any data transfers occur directly between your device and your Jellyfin server.

Legal Compliance

This app complies with:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Other applicable privacy laws

Your Consent

By using Finzy, you consent to this privacy policy and the processing of your information as described herein.
